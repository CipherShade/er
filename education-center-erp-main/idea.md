## ERP system for educational centers in egypt (نظام إدارة سناتر الدروس الخصوصية)
This is a project or an ERP system for educational centers in Egypt that will replace papers and manual documentation for students' names, teachers' calendars, and manual financial handling of revenue and expenses with a full, modern ERP system covering all operations.

### Key Egyptian Localization & Arabic Version
The system is built **Arabic-first with native RTL (Right-to-Left) design** and Egyptian center terminology (سناتر، حصص، بوفيه، ملازم، تصفية حسابات). Front-desk receptionists operate seamlessly in Arabic with sub-second student lookup that tolerates Arabic typing variations (like أ/إ/آ and ة/ه).

## How this works 
Every student will come to the center and tell the receptionist which subject they have with which teacher now. The person in the lobby opens the system, selects one of the active sessions currently running or starting soon, clicks to search or quick-add the student, collects the session fee (via Cash, Vodafone Cash, or InstaPay), and instantly checks them in. At the end of the session, the system reconciles lobby attendance with the teacher assistant's roll call and automatically calculates the center's fixed cut vs. the teacher's cash payout.
